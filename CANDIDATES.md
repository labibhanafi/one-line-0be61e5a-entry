# Candidates — One Line, One Laugh

Scored against the judge's five criteria, in order (5 points each, 25 max):
1. whole, self-contained funny line; 2. real laugh / turn; 3. tight;
4. fresh; 5. clear on first read. Target length 80–130 characters;
gate is 1–140 characters, one sentence.

## A — standing desk — 23/25 (94 chars)

> I bought a standing desk to be healthier, and now I just sit on a
> taller chair in front of it.

Whole 5, laugh 4, tight 5, fresh 4, clear 5. Solid self-defeating loop,
but "taller chair" is a mild visual and standing-desk jokes are a known
genre.

## B — weather app — 24/25 (97 chars)

> I deleted social media to save time, and now I just check the weather
> app like it owes me gossip.

Whole 5, laugh 5, tight 5, fresh 5, clear 4. "Owes me gossip" is a vivid
punch; the personification costs half a beat to parse.

## C — plastic plant — 24/25 (93 chars)

> My plant died, so I bought a plastic one, and now I water it every
> morning out of pure guilt.

Whole 5, laugh 5, tight 4, fresh 5, clear 5. Absurd care for a fake plant;
"every morning" and "pure" are both softeners — the 74-char trimmed
version is tighter.

## D — meal prep — 24/25 (87 chars)

> I tried meal prepping and now I have seven identical containers of
> regret in my fridge.

Whole 5, laugh 5, tight 5, fresh 4, clear 5. "Containers of regret" is a
strong final phrase; the meal-prep-fail genre itself is familiar.

## E — calendar — 25/25 (91 chars) — PICKED

> My calendar reminded me to relax, so I scheduled it for next month and
> marked it tentative.

Whole 5, laugh 5, tight 5, fresh 5, clear 5. The only 25/25: a double
turn — relaxation is first pushed to next month, then downgraded to
"tentative" — so even rest gets bureaucratised. Sharpest final beat of
all seven candidates.

## F — password manager — 21/25 (107 chars)

> My password manager forgot my password, so now the thing I bought to
> remember everything remembers nothing.

Whole 5, laugh 4, tight 4, fresh 3, clear 5. The loop is clean but the
middle ("the thing I bought to remember everything") is wordy and the
premise is a familiar genre.

## G — smartwatch stress — 22/25 (97 chars)

> My smartwatch says I'm stressed, so now I'm stressed about being
> stressed, which it also noticed.

Whole 5, laugh 4, tight 5, fresh 3, clear 5. The recursive twist works,
but the stress-loop shape is a known joke form.
